# Why OpenMarket Matters

## The Problem
When a new drug launches, three things determine commercial success: the price the market will bear, who already occupies the disease space, and what the label allows the team to say. Today, that intelligence is locked behind expensive subscriptions (IQVIA, Evaluate, GlobalData) or buried in slow consulting decks. Small biotechs and academic spinouts often launch products without ever seeing this data.

## What OpenMarket Does
Combines public-domain sources — FDA Orange Book, FDA approvals, OpenFDA AERS, drug labels, public pricing — into a dashboard. Any drug team can compare assets, see the competitive landscape, and generate a commercial brief in a single click.

## Who Benefits
- Small-cap pharma without dedicated commercial teams
- Biotech founders preparing for partnering or financing discussions
- BD teams scoping deals
- Investors doing diligence on listed pharma

## Why Now
OpenFDA, RxNorm, and DailyMed have matured into reliable APIs. Cross-walking drug names across sources — historically the hardest step — is now solvable with LLM-assisted normalization.

## The Bigger Picture
Lower-cost commercial intelligence levels the playing field. The next generation of innovative drugs is increasingly coming from small companies — and those companies need affordable tools to make commercially-literate decisions before they can afford a commercial leadership team.
